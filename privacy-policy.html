Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

IM NEXUS ("we," "us," "our") operates the website at imnexus.site. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us directly, such as when you fill out a quote request, contact form, or client review form on this site. This may include your name, email address, phone number, business name, and details about your project.

Our contact and quote forms are processed through Netlify Forms, our website hosting provider's built-in form-handling service. When you submit a form, that information is sent to Netlify's servers and then to us. We do not use the information you submit for any purpose other than responding to your enquiry and delivering the service you requested.

WhatsApp and Third-Party Links

This website includes links and buttons that open WhatsApp so you can message us directly. If you use these links, your conversation with us takes place on WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms, which are set by Meta, not by IM NEXUS.

This site also links to our social media profiles (Facebook, TikTok). Interactions on those platforms are governed by each platform's own privacy policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries, quote requests, and project discussions
  • Deliver the design, print, web, or creative services you request
  • Maintain records of client communication for ongoing projects
  • Improve our services and how we present them on this website

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This website may use minimal, functional cookies necessary for the site to work correctly. We do not currently run third-party advertising or analytics tracking that profiles individual visitors. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry and project information for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver our services, maintain business records, and meet any legal or accounting obligations. You may request that we delete information you've submitted, subject to any records we're required to keep by law.

Your Rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. IM NEXUS operates from Ghana and handles personal data in line with the principles of Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business owners and organisations seeking creative and technology services. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as our website and services evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
