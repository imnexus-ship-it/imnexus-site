A complete brand system for a US-based African cuisine restaurant — logo, menus, promotional flyers, product labels, signage and merchandise, all designed to feel like one consistent brand.

The brief

One brand, applied everywhere it needed to show up.

Adoma's needed their brand to work as hard on a product label as it does on a promotional flyer or a storefront sign — while staying recognizably "Adoma's" wherever a customer encountered it. We built the logo first, then applied it consistently across every touchpoint below.
